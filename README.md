drawn as a dense, dramatic front page: a big masthead, a drawn lead
scene of a tanker burning in the Strait of Hormuz, a two-line lead
headline, six more stories in a grid, and an ALSO TODAY strip for the
rest of the day.

## How it is made

`generate.py` draws the 1024x1024 front page with Pillow and DejaVu
fonts (deterministic: two runs are byte-identical). `main.py` is
standard-library only and copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
